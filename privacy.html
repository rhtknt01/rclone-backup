Privacy policy for "rclone backup"

"rclone backup" is a private, personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

The tool uploads backup copies of the owner's own files from the owner's computer to the owner's own Google Drive. Through Google's "drive.file" permission it can only see and change the files it uploaded itself.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data from any other person.
  • It does not share, sell or send any data to anyone. Files go only from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive.
  • It has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where data is kept

The files stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the backup files in Google Drive.
